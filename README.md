A 3D open-world crime game set in a bayou-and-highway frontier city. Run moonshine-and-muscle-car jobs for swamp patriarch Tío Braulio Fuentes: pilot a fan-driven airboat through the channels of Cañaveral Swamp, outrun the Patrulla Estatal on a long truck-filled interstate, and export 10 named muscle cars from the dock at Faros Point.

## Features (true numbers)

- 5 named districts: Puerto Lodo, The Interstate, Cañaveral Swamp, Villa Ceniza, Faros Point
- 7 staged story missions with portrait dialogue, stage objectives and MISSION PASSED payouts
- Pilotable airboat that slides on water and mud and can beach on mud banks
- Drivable, stealable cars with witness calls; highway chase missions with truck traffic
- Wanted 0–5 stars, named Patrulla Estatal units, roadblocks, and the GARZA air unit at 4+ stars
- 12 stunt ramps, 10 export-list muscle cars, collectible moonshine jugs
- Full HUD: mission panel, live dispatch, weapon wheel, round minimap, RESPECT bar, radio, vehicle panel
- Full map (M) with GPS route, stats/save screen with 3 localStorage save slots
- Golden-hour grade plus night mode, WebAudio synth sound, deterministic seeded world
- Autopilot proof: open `game/index.html?autopilot=1`

## Controls

- WASD / arrows — move / drive · Shift — sprint · Space — brake / jump
- E — enter / steal car, board airboat, talk, deliver
- F / mouse — aim & shoot · 1–7 — weapon wheel · Z / X / C — crew abilities
- M — full map · P / Tab — stats & saves · N — night mode · R — radio station

## Run

Serve `game/` statically (e.g. `python3 -m http.server` inside `game/`) and open `index.html`.
